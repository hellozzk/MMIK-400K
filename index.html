01 / OVERVIEW

A benchmark for anomaly cognition

Abstract

Industrial inspection requires more than locating unusual pixels. A useful system must understand normal visual rules, identify deviations, and explain their significance. MMIK-400K connects scene knowledge pretraining, anomaly understanding, and open-ended cognition in one benchmark. ADSeeker++ combines ADAgent’s normal-to-anomaly evidence with an Image-RAG normal reference, then uses a vision-language model to answer inspection questions.

03 / METHOD

ADSeeker++

ADAgent models normal-to-anomaly relations within the query. Image-RAG retrieves a relevant normal reference using ColQwen similarity and optional KDE-GMM selection. Qwen3-VL reasons over the query, reference, heatmap, and question.

01

Internal knowledge

ADAgent exposes anomalous regions through a heatmap.

02

External knowledge

Image-RAG selects a normal reference from the candidate pool.

Question answering
